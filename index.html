📐 参考量怎么算

每个人在「我的家庭」点自己名字旁的「⚖️ 体重」填上公斤数，就按体重算：

每天热量 ≈ 体重(公斤) × 30 千卡
每天蛋白质 ≈ 体重(公斤) × 1 克（普通成年人）

例如 60 公斤：约 1800 千卡、60 克蛋白质。没填体重的按成年人平均 2000 千卡、60 克算。全家参考量是每个人加起来。

蛋白质按普通成年人的标准 1.0 克/公斤 算。（经常健身增肌的人一般会用到 1.6 克/公斤，仅供参考。）

这是轻体力活动成年人的大致估算（参考《中国居民膳食指南》）；孩子、孕期、运动量大或有特殊健康情况的，请以医生或营养师建议为准。

Each person taps “⚖️ Weight” next to their name on the Family tab. The daily reference is then based on body weight:

Calories ≈ weight (kg) × 30 kcal
Protein ≈ weight (kg) × 1 g (typical adult)

For example, 60 kg (132 lb) ≈ 1,800 kcal and 60 g of protein. Without a weight, we use an adult average of 2,000 kcal and 60 g. The family reference is everyone added together.

Protein uses the typical-adult standard of 1.0 g per kg. (People who train to build muscle often aim for about 1.6 g per kg — for reference only.)

This is a rough estimate for lightly active adults. Children, pregnancy, heavy exercise or health conditions call for advice from a doctor or dietitian.

❓ 使用说明

👨‍👩‍👧 1. 全家加入同一个冰箱

第一个人点「创建我们家的冰箱」，起个名字（在英文界面点「✏️ Rename」可以另起一个英文名，英文界面就显示英文名）。其他家人打开网址后，在欢迎窗里输入「我的家庭」页黄色卡片上的 Family ID（10 位字母和数字）加入，再选自己的称呼。家庭编号就像家里的钥匙，只发给家人。

📱 2. 放到手机桌面

iPhone：用 Safari 打开 → 点「分享」→「添加到主屏幕」。安卓：用 Chrome 打开 → 右上角「⋮」→「添加到主屏幕」。不要用无痕模式。从桌面图标第一次打开时，如果又出现欢迎窗，输入家庭编号、点自己的名字认回来就行。

📅 3. 排一周食谱

在「本周食谱」里给每天的早餐、午餐、晚餐点「＋ 加菜」，从家常菜里选，或自己写菜名和食材（如：羊肉 1斤，葱 1把；如果菜本身就是要买的东西，食材栏只写数量也行，如菜名"鸡蛋"、食材"4个"）。冰箱里没有的食材会自动放进购买清单；删掉一道菜，不再需要的食材也会自动去掉。米饭现做现吃，不会自动加。

🛒 4. 买菜

「我的家庭」页的购买清单可以自己添加（写上数量，如：猪肉 1斤、牛奶 1加仑、牛肉 2磅；也可以写英文，如 milk 1 gallon、12 eggs、2 lb beef）。买到一样就勾选一样，它会直接放进冰箱，并按食材自动算好能放几天；勾错了点提示里的「撤销」就放回清单。

不要的条目点右边的 ✕，直接删除。食谱自动加的删掉后不会再自动加回来，除非以后又排了用到它的新菜。

在「📏 常用单位」里选美国，食谱自动加的牛奶、果汁用加仑，肉菜用磅；选中国用盒和斤。

🧊 5. 冰箱

快过期的排在最前面，红色表示 2 天内到期。保存天数参考美国农业部 FoodKeeper，有包装保质期的以包装为准，点「剩 X 天 ✏️」可以改；肉类可以点「❄️ 冻起来」，能放更久（冷冻的是浅蓝底）。盐、蚝油、酱油等放在最下面的「🧂 调料」区，用完点「用完了」。

🍽️ 6. 记录吃了多少

在冰箱里点食材右边绿色的「吃」，选全部、一半或自己填数量。没吃完的会留在冰箱里（卡片变成浅橙色），营养只按吃掉的量算。买错或扔掉的点「🗑 删除」，数量不对点「✏️ 改数量」（如 1箱、6瓶）。

📊 7. 营养大盘

谁在自己手机上点「吃」，就记在谁名下。每个人在「我的家庭」点「⚖️ 体重」填上体重，参考量就按体重算：热量 ≈ 体重(公斤) × 30 千卡，蛋白质 ≈ 体重(公斤) × 1 克（普通成年人标准）。体重可以写公斤或斤（如 55公斤 或 110斤）。记错的记录点左边的 ✕ 删除。数值是估算的参考值。

⚠️ 免责声明

本 App 的保存天数、热量和蛋白质都是参考值：保存天数参考美国农业部 FoodKeeper 食品保存指南（取较短、较安全的天数），营养数值参考常见食物成分表。它们只供家庭日常参考，不构成食品安全、医疗或营养方面的专业建议。食物还能不能吃，请以包装上的保质期和食物的实际状态（气味、颜色、质地）为准，拿不准时宁可丢掉。有过敏、特殊疾病、孕期或婴幼儿饮食等情况，请咨询医生或营养师。

👨‍👩‍👧 1. Join the same fridge

The first person taps “Create our fridge” and gives it a name. Tap “✏️ Rename” in English mode to give it an English name; Chinese screens keep the Chinese name. Everyone else opens the website and, in the welcome window, enters the Family ID (10 letters/numbers) from the yellow card on the Family tab, then picks their name. The Family ID works like a house key — only share it with family.

📱 2. Add it to your home screen

iPhone: open it in Safari → tap Share → “Add to Home Screen”. Android: open it in Chrome → ⋮ → “Add to Home screen”. Don’t use private browsing. The first time you open it from the icon, if the welcome window appears again, enter your Family ID and tap your name.

📅 3. Plan the week’s meals

On the Meals tab, tap “＋ Add” for any breakfast, lunch or dinner. Pick a built-in dish or write your own name and ingredients (e.g. beef 2 lb, carrots 3). If the dish itself is what you buy, you can enter just an amount (e.g. dish “eggs”, ingredients “12”). Anything not in the fridge is added to the shopping list automatically; remove a dish and ingredients nobody needs are taken off again. Cooked rice is never added.

🛒 4. Shopping

Add items to the shopping list on the Family tab, with an amount — e.g. “milk 1 gallon”, “12 eggs”, “2 lb ground beef”, “a dozen eggs”. Chinese works too. Tick each item as you buy it — it goes straight into the fridge with a storage time worked out for that food. Ticked the wrong one? Tap “Undo” in the message.

Tap ✕ to delete an item. Items added by the meal plan won’t come back unless you plan a new dish that needs them.

Under “📏 Units”, choose US to have the meal plan use gallons for milk and pounds for meat and produce, or Metric / China for boxes and 斤.

🧊 5. The fridge

Items expiring soonest are on top; red means 2 days or less. Storage times are based on USDA FoodKeeper — if the package has a date, go by the package. Tap “⏱️ days left ✏️” to change a date. Meat and fish can be frozen with “❄️ Freeze” (frozen items have a blue background). Salt, sauces and oils go in the “🧂 Seasonings” section at the bottom; tap “Used up” when finished.

🍽️ 6. Record what you eat

In the fridge, tap the green “Eat” button and choose all, half or an amount. Anything left stays in the fridge (orange background) and only what you ate counts toward nutrition. Use “🗑 Delete” for things bought by mistake or thrown away, and “✏️ Amount” to correct quantities (e.g. 1 box, 6 bottles).

📊 7. Nutrition

Whoever taps “Eat” on their phone gets the credit. Enter your weight with “⚖️ Weight” on the Family tab and your daily reference is based on it: calories ≈ weight (kg) × 30, protein ≈ weight (kg) × 1 g (typical adult). You can enter kg or lb. Tap ✕ to delete a wrong record. All numbers are estimates.

⚠️ Disclaimer

Storage times, calories and protein in this app are estimates: storage times are based on the USDA FoodKeeper food storage guide (using the shorter, safer end of each range) and nutrition figures on common food composition tables. They are for everyday household reference only and are not professional food-safety, medical or nutrition advice. Whether food is still safe to eat depends on the date on the package and the food’s actual condition (smell, colour, texture) — when in doubt, throw it out. For allergies, medical conditions, pregnancy or infant feeding, consult a doctor or dietitian.

Everything is free. No account needed.
